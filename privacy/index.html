Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

The short version. You don't need an account to play our games, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. Each game gives itself a random player ID on your device. We use it, along with basic gameplay events and crash reports, to keep the games working and make them better. Ads only appear when you choose to watch one, and Apple or Google handle all payments. We don't sell your data.

This policy covers every mobile game published by BonBon Studios on the App Store and Google Play, and this website. BonBon Studios is a trade name of BonBon Games LLC ("we", "us"), a Wyoming limited liability company, which is responsible for your data.

What we collect

Random player ID

When you first open one of our games, it creates a random ID and stores it on your device. The ID isn't based on your name, your device's hardware or any other identifier. Deleting the game deletes the ID. Each game has its own ID.

Gameplay events

The games send short records of what happens while you play, together with the player ID, the game and its version, your platform (iOS or Android) and the time. Examples are a session starting, a level being finished with its score and time, an ad being offered or watched, a product being bought, and a clip being shared. We use these to fix problems, balance difficulty and decide what to make next.

Leaderboards

If a game has a leaderboard, your best scores are saved with your player ID and the display name you choose, up to 20 characters. Other players can see that name and your score, so please don't use your real name or anything private.

Crash and error reports

If a game crashes or hits an error, it sends a technical report: what went wrong in the code, the game and its version, your device model and your operating system version. We use these only to fix bugs.

Purchases

In-app purchases are made through Apple or Google, and we never see your payment details. To unlock what you bought and to let you restore purchases, our purchase service receives the receipt from Apple or Google together with your player ID.

Ads

Our games show ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your IP address, device information and your device's advertising ID, if you've allowed it.

  • On iPhone, the game asks for permission to track before any ad is personalized. If you say no, you still get ads, but they aren't personalized.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears first, and ads are personalized only if you agree.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings at any time.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policy.

Saved games and clips

Your progress and settings are stored on your device. Some games record the last few seconds of play so you can share a moment. That clip stays on your device and only leaves it if you choose to share it.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't build profiles of you across other companies' apps or websites.

How long we keep it

We keep gameplay events and leaderboard entries only as long as we need them to run and improve the games, and we delete data from games we take off the stores. Crash reports are deleted automatically after the crash-reporting service's retention period. If you ask us to delete your data, we delete it.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and aren't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us personal information, for example their real name as a leaderboard name, write to us and we'll delete it.

Gutspin, our slot-machine game, is for adults 18 and over only. It uses play coins that have no cash value and can't be cashed out or transferred, and there are no prizes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy of it, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent. To use any of these rights, email hello@playbonbon.com and tell us the game and, if you can, your player ID (shown in the game's settings) or your leaderboard name. We'll reply within 30 days. If you live in the EU, the UK or Switzerland, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we only know you by a random player ID, deleting a game also cuts the link between you and the data it sent.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and only we can read gameplay data and crash reports. Leaderboards only show names and scores.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new date. If a change is important, we'll also mention it in the games.

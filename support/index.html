Support

We're a tiny studio and we read every message.

Write to us

hello@playbonbon.com. Tell us which game, your phone model and what happened. A screenshot helps. We usually answer within two working days.

I bought something and it's gone

Open the game's Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you used to buy it. If it still doesn't show up, email us with the game's name and the date of the purchase.

Refunds

Apple and Google handle all payments, so refunds go through them:

  • iPhone and iPad: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Android: Google Play refund requests

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your phone. Deleting the game, or clearing its storage, deletes your progress too. If you lost progress any other way, email us and we'll look into it.

Ads

Our games only show an ad when you tap to watch one for a reward. If an ad didn't give you the reward, tell us which game and roughly when it happened.

Delete my data

Email hello@playbonbon.com with the game's name and, if you can, your player ID (in the game's settings) or your leaderboard name. We'll delete your gameplay data and leaderboard entries within 30 days and confirm by email. You don't need an account for any of our games, so there's no account to close. The privacy policy explains what we keep.

Gutspin

Gutspin is a slot-machine game for adults 18 and over. Its coins are play money with no cash value: they can't be cashed out, transferred or exchanged for prizes. Winning in Gutspin doesn't mean you'd win at real gambling. If gambling is a problem for you or someone you know, call or text the US National Problem Gambling Helpline at 1-800-GAMBLER.

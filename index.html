How we work

One joke per game

Every game starts as one silly sentence. A floor scrubber doing backflips. A guard who throws you out the window. If it doesn't fit in a sentence, it isn't ready.

One seagull beats two

Every picture uses the fewest things that tell the joke. Empty space is part of the gag.

Played on a real phone

Every build goes onto a real iPhone before it goes anywhere near you. If it feels wrong under a thumb, it gets fixed.

If it's annoying, it goes

We'd rather delete a feature than patch it forever. Ask the tunnels.

Music from the old masters

Our soundtracks are public-domain classics we arrange ourselves: Offenbach's Galop Infernal, the Radetzky March, In the Hall of the Mountain King.

Free, no account

Every game is free to download and you never sign up. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number. Our privacy policy says exactly what we collect.
